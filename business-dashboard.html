Tips to Get More Responses

Make the most of your auto-reply

  • ⚡
    Keep it under 160 characters Shorter messages have higher open and reply rates. Get straight to the point.
  • ❓
    End with a question Ask "How can we help?" to invite the customer to reply and start a conversation.
  • 🙋
    Use a first name if possible Personalized messages feel more human. Add your name to build trust fast.
  • 🕐
    Mention your hours Tell customers when you'll call back. It sets expectations and reduces frustration.
