SMS Messaging Consent Policy

How LeadReply handles automated SMS messages, customer consent, and opt-out rights

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

📱 Important Notice for Callers

If you have called a business that uses LeadReply and received an automated text message, this page explains your rights, how to opt out, and how our messaging service works.

1. How Customer Consent Is Obtained

LeadReply operates a missed call auto-reply SMS service for local businesses. Our service works as follows:

  • A customer calls a business phone number that is registered with LeadReply
  • The call goes unanswered (the business does not pick up)
  • LeadReply automatically sends one SMS message to the caller on behalf of the business
  • The SMS is sent as a direct response to the caller's own inbound call attempt

✅ Consent method: End-users provide implicit consent by placing a phone call to a LeadReply-registered business number. By initiating the call, the customer has expressed intent to communicate with that business. The automated SMS is sent solely as a response to their attempted contact. No unsolicited messages are ever sent to any number that has not first called a registered business.

This is classified as implicit opt-in consent — the customer initiates contact by calling, and our automated text reply is a direct response to that contact attempt. No prior registration, form submission, or verbal agreement is required because the customer's own phone call constitutes their intent to communicate.

4. Opt-Out Rights

You have the right to opt out of receiving SMS messages from any LeadReply-registered business at any time. To opt out:

  • Reply STOP to any message you receive — this immediately unsubscribes you from that specific business number
  • You will receive a confirmation message: "You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe."
  • No further messages will be sent from that business number to your phone number
  • To re-enable messages, reply START at any time
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@leadreplygroup.com

5. No Unsolicited Messages

LeadReply does not send unsolicited SMS messages. Our system is designed with the following safeguards:

  • Messages are only sent to phone numbers that have first called a registered business number
  • Only one automated reply is sent per missed call
  • Numbers on the opt-out list never receive messages from that business again
  • We do not purchase, rent, or use third-party marketing lists
  • We do not send promotional or marketing messages to numbers that have not initiated contact
